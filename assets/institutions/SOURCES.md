# Institutional identification assets

`csu.png` is the Central South University emblem reproduced unmodified from `images/csu.png` in the public CSU thesis template:
https://github.com/CSUcse/CSUthesis/blob/b94763195fdb0ec4c7fc3760b4426b81fab31865/images/csu.png

The emblem identifies university affiliations and links to https://www.csu.edu.cn/. The template is community-maintained, not an official university website; use of the emblem does not imply endorsement. The mark remains the university's property.

`briefcase.svg` and `research.svg` are original generic category icons, not company logos. No Hunan Jiace domain or corporate logo has been guessed. Its links lead to the site's detailed internship entry. BROAD is linked by name to https://www.broad.com/; external corporate/university domains could not be fetched under the cloud network policy. Professor names link to their institution, not an unverified personal profile.

`columbia.svg` reproduces the Columbia crown mark from the public `jmd2296/Columbia-Logo` repository at commit `baeabc25bc6cd4abcc342692c43844e143d6bec2`, file `Logo_Tilt (1).svg`. The source is community-hosted, not an official university asset distribution. The source's rotated mark is displayed upright via CSS; the SVG itself is unmodified. It identifies the university and links to https://www.columbia.edu/; it does not imply endorsement.
