ReSynth: Verified Synthetic Data for Multi-Image Understanding

Research · Multimodal alignment

Hallucination-Aligned Visual Negative Construction for Multimodal DPO

Built an offline preference-data pipeline using model rollouts, language-model judging, and image generation. MMHal-Bench improved from 3.82 to 3.99; hallucination rate fell from 37.6% to 33.3%. Benchmark trade-offs and evaluation settings are documented on the project page.

Research · Trustworthy AI

Mitigating Visual Hallucination in VQA for MLLMs

Constructed positive and negative training pairs with region masking and caption-based supervision. Reduced the dataset from 265K to 163K examples without accuracy loss, as reported in the CV.

Vision-language models · VQAResearch project

Research · Data selection

Data Selection for GRPO-based MLLM Training

Developed advantage-based filtering to assess training-data utility, identifying roughly 50% of examples as ineffective and a high-value subset of approximately 5%. Built a vectorized Parquet pipeline for reproducible selection.

Research · Training strategy

Curriculum Learning for Multimodal Models

Evaluated 20 SFT checkpoints and constructed a 72K-example reasoning curriculum ranked by Pass@5 difficulty. Explored Naive GRPO and CoT Rollout GRPO with hint fading; early experiments indicated faster convergence.
