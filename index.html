About me

I am currently an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Columbia University. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Technology with First-Class Honours from Central South University.

At Central South University, I work with Prof. Jinpeng Wang in the CSU-JPG Group. I have also collaborated with Prof. Lei Wang and Prof. Min Li. In Prof. Lei Wang’s group, I worked on a university–industry collaboration with Hunan Jiace Test to develop a multimodal verification system for software acceptance reports. The system is now in use in a factory.

My research interests include vision-language models, data-centric AI, and reinforcement learning. My recent work focuses on multimodal reasoning, visual hallucination, preference optimization, and verified synthetic data for more reliable multimodal systems.
