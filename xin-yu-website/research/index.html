01 / SELECTED WORK
Under review · ICLR 2027 submission

ReSynth: Verified Synthetic Data for Multi-Image Understanding

A relation-based generation and visual-verification pipeline spanning five multi-image reasoning abilities. A 13K-example synthetic corpus was used to train Qwen2.5-VL-7B with GRPO; the reported +4.25-point average gain covers BLINK, MUIRBench, and VLM2-Bench. The +9.75-point counting gain is attributed to visual verification.

Selected research

01

Visual grounding

Mitigating Visual Hallucination in VQA for MLLMs

Constructed positive/negative pairs by masking target regions and used caption-based supervision to distinguish visible evidence. Filtered 265K examples to 163K without accuracy loss, as reported in the CV.

02

Training data

Data Selection for GRPO-based MLLM Training

Developed advantage-based sample filtering and a vectorized Parquet processing pipeline. The CV reports roughly half of examples as ineffective and a high-value subset of approximately 5%.

03

Learning strategies

Curriculum Learning Strategies for MLLM Training

Evaluated 20 SFT checkpoints and built a 72K-example curriculum ranked by Pass@5 difficulty. Explored Naive GRPO and CoT Rollout GRPO with hint fading; early experiments indicated faster convergence than the baseline.
